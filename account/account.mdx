Open your account menu, then **Account** (or **Account Settings**).

## How do I update my profile?

1. Open **Account**.
2. Edit **First name** and **Last name**.
3. **Email** is read-only.
4. **Mobile phone** saves as you change it.

To switch accounts, use [Advanced](/account/advanced) when you belong to more than one account.
