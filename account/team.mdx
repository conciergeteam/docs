---
title: "Team"
description: "Invite associates and set admin, team member, and inbox-only roles."
---

Open **Team** under account settings (`/settings/team`).

## How do I add someone to my team?

1. Click **Invite Team Member**.
2. Complete the invite (including role).
3. Send the invite.

On a pending row, **Resend invite** sends the email again.

## What's the difference between an admin and a regular team member?

- **Admin** — full event and account settings, including **Export**.
- **Team Member** — standard event work (people, lists, broadcasts, inbox, website, most settings).
- **Messages Only** — People and Inbox only.
- **Assigned Only** — People and Inbox only, focused on assigned conversations.

## How do I remove someone from the team?

1. Open the row menu.
2. Click **Remove**.
3. Or click **Edit**, change the role, then **Save** / **Cancel**.
