title: "Advanced"
description: "Switch between accounts you belong to."
---

**Advanced** appears only when you belong to more than one account.

## How do I switch to a different account?

1. Open **Advanced**.
2. Use **Switch Account** and pick the account.

**Add Account** creates another account association (**Add Account** / **Cancel**).

Integrations and account-level phone numbers are not shown in account settings.
