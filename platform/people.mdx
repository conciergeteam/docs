---
title: "People"
description: "Add, import, search, and organize people for an event."
---

The **People** page is the directory of everyone on this event. Open it from **People** in the sidebar.

Each person can have a name, mobile phone number, SMS subscription status, lists, assignment to a team member, and any custom properties your event tracks.

Associates with the **Messages Only** or **Assigned Only** role can still open People. **Lists** and **Broadcasts** are hidden for those roles.

## How do I add a person?

1. Open **People**.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Click **New person to this event**.
4. Enter **First name**, **Last name**, and **Phone number**.
5. If you want them on broadcasts and the **All** list, leave **Subscribe this phone number to broadcast messages and add this person to the All list** checked.
6. Click **Save**.

## How do I add people from a spreadsheet?

1. Open **People**.
2. Click **Import**.
3. On **Import People**, choose a destination:
   - **Just into this event** — import into the event (not a single list).
   - **Into a specific list...** — pick a list, then click **Next**.
4. Click **+ Upload** or **Choose File** and select a CSV, XLS, or XLSX file. You can **Download Import Template** first if you need a sample.
5. On **Map Headers** (or **Map Columns**), match spreadsheet columns to person fields, then click **Continue** or **Next**.
6. On **Preview Import**, review the rows, then click **Import Selected**.
7. Confirm the result on **Import Confirmation**.

During import, people can be placed on lists from the file or from the list you chose as the destination.

## How do I put someone in a list?

1. Open **People**.
2. Check the box next to one or more people.
   You can also open a person's row menu (⋮) and click **Add to a list**.
3. Click **Add to List**.
4. Search or select a list, or click **New List**, enter a name, and click **Save**.
5. Click **Add to list**.

To add people while you are already viewing one list, use **Filter by list**, then **Add** → **People to this list**, search by name, check people, and click **Add to list**.

## How do I find people whose texts aren't going through?

1. Open **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Use the tabs for the issue type: **No Phone Number**, **Invalid Phone**, **Landline**, **International**, **Not Yet Subscribed**, or **Unsubscribed**.
3. Click a name to open their profile, or **Edit** to update their phone number.

You can also search on **People** with **Search by...** (for example **First** or **Last**) and review subscription badges on each profile.

## How do I search and filter people?

1. Open **People**.
2. Use **Filter by list** to show one list, or leave it on everyone.
3. Use **Search by...** to pick a field, then type in the search box.
4. Optional: click **Show/Hide Columns** to choose table columns.
5. If guest assignment is enabled for your account, use **Assigned To** to filter by **Unassigned**, **Me**, or a team member.

## How do I message people from this page?

- One person: row menu (⋮) → **Send Message**, or select exactly one row and click **Send Message**.
- Everyone on a list: **Filter by list**, then click **Message this list** (no search or extra filters).

## How do I assign people to a team member?

This control appears when guest assignment is enabled.

1. Open **People**.
2. Select one or more people.
3. Click **Assign To**.
4. Choose **Unassigned**, **Me**, or a team member.

You can also assign from a person's profile with **Assigned To**.

## How do I archive a person?

Archiving is on the person's edit form, not the People table. See [Person](/platform/person#how-do-i-archive-a-person).
