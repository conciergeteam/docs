---

**Directory** is under **Event Website** when the guest directory is enabled for the event (`bio-book`).

If the directory is off, the page links you to **Directory settings**.

## How do I show or hide people in the directory?

1. Open **Directory**.
2. Use **Visible** and **Not Visible**.
3. **Search people** if the list is long (**Clear search** to reset).
4. Select people and click **Hide from directory** or **Show in directory**.

## How do I preview the guest directory?

Click **Guest View** when a directory URL exists. **Directory Settings** opens the settings tab for URL, password, and enablement. See [Directory settings](/platform/settings/directory).
