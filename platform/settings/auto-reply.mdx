---
title: "Auto-reply"
description: "Send an automatic reply when someone texts the event."
---

Open **Event Settings** → **Auto-Reply**.

## How do I send an automatic reply when someone texts in?

1. Open **Auto-Reply**.
2. Enter or **Edit** the auto-reply message.
3. Optional: **Only send auto-replies during specific hours**.
4. Click **Turn On** or **Save**.
5. **Turn Off** stops auto-replies. **Cancel** discards an in-progress edit.
