---
title: "Dates and times"
description: "Set event dates and how dates and times display."
---

Open **Event Settings** → **Dates & Times**.

## How do I update the event's time range?

1. Set **Start Date** and **End Date**.
2. Choose **Date Format**.
3. Choose **Time Format**: **2:30 PM** or **14:30**.
4. Click **Save**.

The event **time zone** is on [Name and location](/platform/settings/name-location). Broadcast send times use this event time zone.
