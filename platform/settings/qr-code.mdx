title: "QR code"
description: "Download a QR code that points guests at this event's text number."
---

Open **Event Settings** → **QR Code**. This tab is hidden on WhatsApp-only events.

## How do I get a QR code for the event number?

1. Open **QR Code** (the event must have a phone number).
2. Customize the generator if needed.
3. Click **Download QR Code**.
