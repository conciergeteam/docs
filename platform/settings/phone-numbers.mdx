---
title: "Event phone numbers"
description: "See extra country numbers on this event."
---

Open **Event Settings** → **Phone Numbers**. This tab only appears when the event has country phone numbers.

## How do I view international numbers?

1. Open **Phone Numbers** in event settings, or click the globe next to the event number in the sidebar (label **International Numbers**).
2. Review the numbers listed for this event.

Account-level phone number management is not shown in account settings.
