Open **Event Settings** → **People Properties**.

## How do I add a custom property?

1. Click **New** or **Add a property**.
2. Fill in the field and click **Save** (or **Cancel**).
3. Row menu: **Edit** or **Remove**.

**Import** starts the bio properties spreadsheet wizard (upload, map headers, preview, confirm). Directory card and field pickers use **Add** / **Add field**.
