---
title: "Keywords"
description: "Let guests join by texting a keyword."
---

Open **Event Settings** → **Keywords**.

## How do I add a keyword?

1. Click **New Keyword**.
2. Set the keyword and what should happen (for example add to a list).
3. Save the keyword.

Remove one with **Delete** (or **Cancel** if you are still editing). Guests who text a new keyword can be created as people if they are not already on the event.
