---

Open **Event Settings** → **Name & Location** (the default settings URL).

## How do I update the event name or place?

1. Edit **Name**.
2. Edit **Location**.
3. Click **Time zone**, then **Select time zone** or **change** if needed.
4. Click **Save**.

Start and end dates are on [Dates and times](/platform/settings/dates-times).
