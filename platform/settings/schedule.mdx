Open **Event Settings** → **Schedule**. Session editing stays on [Schedule](/platform/schedule).

## How do I turn on RSVP for sessions?

1. Set **Guest Site URL** (subdomain) if needed.
2. Optional: **Schedule Title Override**.
3. Enable **RSVP**.
4. Under **Publicly visible lists**, use **Add version** as needed.
5. Click **Save Changes**.
