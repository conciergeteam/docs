Open **Event Settings** → **Brand**. This tab is hidden until the event has a guest **subdomain**.

## How do I change guest site branding?

1. Adjust fonts, colors, banners, and title overrides for each section.
2. Click **Save**.
3. Click **Guest View** to preview.
