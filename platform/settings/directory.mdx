---

Open **Event Settings** → **Directory**. Who is listed is managed on [Directory](/platform/directory).

## How do I enable the guest directory?

1. Turn on **Enable guest directory**.
2. Set **Directory URL**.
3. Optional: guest site menu toggle, and **Password Protection** (**Save** / **Cancel**).
4. Click **Save** on the page.
