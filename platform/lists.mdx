---
title: "Lists"
description: "Create and manage lists so you can message and organize groups of people."
---

The **Lists** page is where you create named collections of people. Open it from **Lists** in the sidebar.

Lists are reused for broadcasts, schedule audiences, FAQ visibility, and keyword join flows. The page subtitle talks about **groups**; in the rest of the app the control is always **list**.

**Lists** is hidden for **Messages Only** and **Assigned Only** roles.

## How do I create a new list?

1. Open **Lists**.
2. Click **New** (or **New List** on a phone).
3. Type the list name.
4. Click **Save**.

You can also create a list from **People** when you add people to a list: click **New List**, name it, and **Save**.

## How do I add a bunch of people to a list at once?

From **Lists**:

1. Open the row menu (⋮) on the list.
2. Click **Add people** to jump to People with that list selected, then add people from the drawer.
3. Or click **Import people into this list**, upload a file, map **First Name** and **Last Name**, preview, and click **Add to list**.

From **People**, filter to the list, click **Add** → **People to this list**, check people, and click **Add to list**.

## How do I send a text to everyone in this list?

1. Open **Lists**.
2. Open the row menu (⋮) on the list.
3. Click **Message this list**.
4. Compose the broadcast with that list already selected. See [Broadcasts](/platform/broadcasts).

On **People**, filter to the list and click **Message this list**.

## How do I rename, duplicate, or archive a list?

1. Open **Lists**.
2. Open the row menu (⋮) on the list.
3. Choose:
   - **View** — open People filtered to this list.
   - **Rename** — edit the name, then **Save**.
   - **Duplicate this list** — confirm **Duplicate List**, then **Save**.
   - **Archive** — confirm **Archive List**, then **Archive**.

Archived lists stay available under **Archived**. Click **Show**, open the row menu, and click **Unarchive** to restore.

## How do I search lists?

1. Open **Lists**.
2. Type in **Search by name**.

Click a list name to open **People** filtered to that list.
