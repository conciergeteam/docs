title: "Events"
description: "Open, create, pin, and archive events from the Events home page."
---

The **Events** page is the home screen when you are not inside an event. Open it from **Events** in the sidebar (or go to `/`).

Each card is one event. Click a card (or the card menu **Open**) to enter that event on **People**.

## How do I start a new event?

1. On **Events**, click **New Event**.
   You can also go to `/events/new`, which opens the same **New Event** panel.
2. Enter **Name**, then click **Save**.
3. Optional: search **Where will the event be held?** (`City, state or venue`), then **Select**, **Skip**, or **Preview**.
4. Confirm **Time Zone** (**change** or **Select a time zone** if needed).
5. Set **Start Date** and **End Date**.
6. Click **Save**, then **Go to event**.

If you have no events yet, the empty state is **No events** with **New Event**.

## How do I find or sort events?

1. Type in **Search by name**.
2. Click **Sort** and choose **A → Z**, **Z → A**, or newest/oldest by created date or start date.

## How do I pin or archive an event?

1. Open the card menu (⋮).
2. Click **Pin to top** or **Unpin**.
3. Or click **Archive**, confirm **Archive Event**, then **Archive**.

To restore an event, expand **Archived**, open the row menu, and click **Unarchive**.
