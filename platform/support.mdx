**Support** is under **Event Website** when the event has a guest site subdomain.

## How do I edit guest support copy?

1. Open **Support**.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Change **Support text**.
4. Click **Save**, or **Cancel**.
5. **Use default** restores the default support text.

**Guest View** (when a subdomain exists) opens the guest support page.
